쓰기

Write

Trace letters in the right stroke order, type Korean on the 2-beolsik keyboard, type the words you hear, or race the clock for 60 seconds.

0words100%accuracy0keys / min60seconds left

Use your normal keyboard: the keys map to the Korean 2-beolsik layout, so no Korean keyboard setting is needed. The next key glows. Shift gives ㄲ ㄸ ㅃ ㅆ ㅉ ㅒ ㅖ. Backspace undoes one key.

연습

Practice

Short drills for the tricky parts of Korean: numbers, verb endings, particles, graded stories, role-play conversations and the sound changes between spelling and speech. Each right answer is +1 XP; wrong ones come back in Mistakes.

시험

Practice test

20 mixed questions in TOPIK style: vocabulary, listening and grammar. 10 minutes, no hints until the end. Pass mark 60%.
