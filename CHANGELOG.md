# Changelog

What changed in each version of Hangul Learner. Newest first. Installed apps update themselves; Settings › Updates shows your version.

## 1.7.3

- **4,320 words, four times as many:** 165 new word units across all six TOPIK levels, about the size of a full learner word list. Counters, colors, jobs, countries and question words for beginners, up to law, medicine, economics, idioms, proverbs and 사자성어 at level 6. Every word is recorded, slowly too and in both voices.
- **Every possible syllable is recorded:** all 11,172 Hangul syllables (가 to 힣) now have a real voice, so anything you build in the syllable builder is said by 선희, and on a computer without a Korean voice your own words are said syllable by syllable.
- **Verb practice knows more irregular verbs:** ㅂ verbs like 새롭다, 그립다 and 어지럽다 now conjugate correctly (새로워요), while 입다 and 잡다 keep their ㅂ (입어요).
- **Exam countdown:** when there are more units left than days, it says how many units a day you need.
- **Faster Words page** with thousands of words: only the words on screen are drawn.

## 1.7.2

- **Practice › Casual:** 79 pairs of textbook and everyday Korean, both ways: short forms (무엇을 → 뭘, 그런데 → 근데), talking with friends (안녕하세요 → 안녕, 밥 먹었어?), slang (알바, 셀카, 꿀잼, 치맥) and written vs spoken (합니다 → 해요, 및 → 하고).
- **Every common syllable is recorded:** all 2,350 syllables Korean uses every day now have a real voice, so the syllable builder and your own words never fall back to a computer voice.
- **Male voice:** Settings › Sound › Voice lets 인준 say letters, syllables and words (선희 is the default).
- **Words light up as they are said** in stories and conversations.
- **Your own shows:** add any podcast (its RSS link) or YouTube channel on the Listen page; remove it from its page.
- **Download progress:** the ↓ button shows how far an episode is, and tapping it again stops the download.
- **More menu:** in narrow windows the bottom bar shows Learn, Words, Practice and Listen, and More opens the other pages.

## 1.7.1

- **Practice › Pairs:** hear syllables learners mix up (가 / 카 / 까, 어 / 오 / 우, 우 / 으, 이 / 에) and pick the one you heard, with a tip on how each is said.
- **Podcasts remember each episode:** an episode carries on where you stopped it, and finished ones are marked played.
- **Sleep timer:** ☾ on the player stops playback after 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
- **Downloaded shelf:** every downloaded episode is listed on the Podcasts page, also ones that dropped off their show’s latest list; play or delete each one there.
- **By skill on Stats:** vocabulary, listening, grammar and reading over your last practice tests, up or down from the tests before.
- **Fewer evening notifications:** the review reminder stays quiet when the daily reminder is on (it already says how many words are due).
- **Tricky words** answered on the Practice page now count for Smart review, and words Smart review spaces 3+ weeks out count as mastered.
- **Fix:** a download interrupted by closing the app no longer leaves a half file behind.

## 1.7.0

- **TOPIK exam countdown:** set your exam date and level in Settings › Goals; Learn shows the days left and the pace (about 1 unit every N days).
- **Daily goal:** choose 10, 30, 50 or 100 XP a day (Settings › Goals).
- **Daily mix:** one tap on Learn gives 10 mixed questions from every unit you have passed.
- **Offline podcasts:** tap ↓ next to an episode to keep it; it plays without internet. Manage them in Settings › Downloads.
- **Look up anything:** a Naver Dictionary button on word cards, and a Papago button next to every story and conversation sentence.
- **Pronunciation check in conversations:** on your turn, Check asks Windows whether it understood your line (needs Windows’ Korean speech pack).
- **Sentence dictation:** Write › Listen now has a “sentences” list for each TOPIK level: hear a whole sentence and type it (letters and spaces, no punctuation).
- **Final consonants (받침):** a new Hangul basics unit on the 7 end sounds, and why 낮, 옷 and 꽃 all end in a “t”.
- **36 new grammar patterns:** a fifth unit for every level: polite and honorific basics, honorific speech (드시다, 계시다, 진지, 연세…), quoting (-냐고/-자고/-라고 하다), passive and causative, and advanced patterns for TOPIK 5–6.
- **8 real-life reading texts:** notices, an ad, a text message, an email, a news item, a timetable and an opinion piece, with 3 questions each.
- **Practice › Reply:** TOPIK-style listening. Hear a line from a conversation and choose the right reply.
- **Typed answers:** Challenge lessons now also ask you to type words from memory on the 2-beolsik keyboard.
- **After each lesson:** the words you missed are listed, to hear again or save to My words.
- **Clearer slow audio:** “Slowly” and Shift+R now play real slow recordings of every letter, syllable and word.
- **Smarter reviews:** one miss no longer sends a well-known word back to day 1, and words learned together no longer all fall due on the same day.
- **Stroke order is now checked:** drawing a stroke backwards or out of order is pointed out.
- **Listening:** 0.5× speed, 3 / 5 / 8-second shadowing pieces, Korean captions on YouTube videos, the podcast goes quiet while a word plays and plays on after a lesson, and the mini player opens where you left it.
- **Safer progress:** an unreadable progress file is kept instead of overwritten, opening the app twice no longer runs two copies, and syncing reads the cloud copy before writing, so your other computer’s progress is merged, never replaced.
- **Fixes:** a downloaded episode resumes from the file after a restart; grammar 보다 (“than”) and the verb 보다 keep separate review schedules; your own words show as text in lessons; Tricky and Reply results survive backups and sync; changing your daily goal no longer changes past days; the reminder no longer comes twice when you change its time; the drawing pad is sharp at 125–200% display scaling; text names macOS and Linux on those systems; links and downloads only use https.

## 1.6.1

- **Mac and Linux versions:** Hangul Learner now also comes for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel; the first launch needs right-click › Open) and Linux (AppImage and .deb). They update themselves like the Windows version.
- On Mac and Linux, the pronunciation check is hidden (it uses Windows speech recognition) and the sync folder is typed in Settings.

## 1.6.0

- **More grammar:** 72 new patterns, so every TOPIK level now has 24 (4 units) instead of 12, each with examples you can hear.
- **Grammar reference:** Words › Grammar lists every pattern with its meaning, how it attaches and examples. Search by pattern, meaning or example.
- **Placement test:** on the Test page (and offered on first launch). It checks one level at a time and starts Learn at the first level that's new to you.
- **Tricky words:** a new Practice tab for words you've missed 3 or more times, with a hint: their Hanja family, their sound in romanization and a look-alike word.
- **Review reminder:** an evening notification when Smart review words are due and you haven't reviewed today (Settings › Notifications).
- **Weekly automatic backup:** a backup file in Documents\Hangul Learner once a week; the last 4 are kept (Settings › Backup).

## 1.5.1

- **YouTube keeps working when its video list is down.** YouTube's list service sometimes fails for every channel; the app now gets the latest videos from YouTube's own player instead. Lists are kept for 6 hours, and the error message no longer blames your internet.
- **Rest days:** finishing all 3 weekly quests earns a rest day (up to 2). A missed day uses one, so your streak continues.
- **Korean text size:** Settings › Text makes Hangul bigger everywhere.
- **Save sentences:** ☆ next to every sentence in stories and conversations saves it to My words and Smart review.
- **Export My words** to a CSV file for Excel or Anki.
- **Press ?** to see every keyboard shortcut.
- Fixed: the Verbs drill's Honorific filter sometimes asked a different ending.

## 1.5.0

- **Sync between PCs** through a OneDrive or Google Drive folder. Studied on two PCs? Both are added together.
- **Clearer voice** at 90% speed, with a male voice for the second speaker in conversations. The download is half the size (25 MB).
- **8 new stories and 4 new conversations** for TOPIK 3–6, and reading questions in practice tests.
- **Hanja word families** on word cards (學 study: 학교, 학생, 대학교…).
- **Konglish unit:** 20 loanwords like 핸드폰, 셀카 and 화이팅.
- **Search by romanization:** "hakgyo" finds 학교.
- **Typing race:** 60 seconds, as many words as you can.
- **Smart review forecast** on the Stats page.
- **Pronunciation check** on word cards, using Windows speech recognition (needs the Korean speech pack in Windows).
- **Weekly report** notification on Sunday evening, and **What's new** after each update.
- The focus timer now rings on time even while the app sits in the tray.
