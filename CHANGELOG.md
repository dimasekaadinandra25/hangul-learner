# Changelog

What changed in each version of Hangul Learner. Newest first. Installed apps update themselves; Settings › Updates shows your version.

## 1.6.1

- **Mac and Linux versions:** Hangul Learner now also comes for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel; the first launch needs right-click › Open) and Linux (AppImage and .deb). They update themselves like the Windows version.
- On Mac and Linux, the pronunciation check is hidden (it uses Windows speech recognition) and the sync folder is typed in Settings.

## 1.6.0

- **More grammar:** 72 new patterns, so every TOPIK level now has 24 (4 units) instead of 12, each with examples you can hear.
- **Grammar reference:** Words › Grammar lists every pattern with its meaning, how it attaches and examples. Search by pattern, meaning or example.
- **Placement test:** on the Test page (and offered on first launch). It checks one level at a time and starts Learn at the first level that's new to you.
- **Tricky words:** a new Practice tab for words you've missed 3 or more times, with a hint: their Hanja family, their sound in romanization and a look-alike word.
- **Review reminder:** an evening notification when Smart review words are due and you haven't reviewed today (Settings › Notifications).
- **Weekly automatic backup:** a backup file in Documents\Hangul Learner once a week; the last 4 are kept (Settings › Backup).

## 1.5.1

- **YouTube keeps working when its video list is down.** YouTube's list service sometimes fails for every channel; the app now gets the latest videos from YouTube's own player instead. Lists are kept for 6 hours, and the error message no longer blames your internet.
- **Rest days:** finishing all 3 weekly quests earns a rest day (up to 2). A missed day uses one, so your streak continues.
- **Korean text size:** Settings › Text makes Hangul bigger everywhere.
- **Save sentences:** ☆ next to every sentence in stories and conversations saves it to My words and Smart review.
- **Export My words** to a CSV file for Excel or Anki.
- **Press ?** to see every keyboard shortcut.
- Fixed: the Verbs drill's Honorific filter sometimes asked a different ending.

## 1.5.0

- **Sync between PCs** through a OneDrive or Google Drive folder. Studied on two PCs? Both are added together.
- **Clearer voice** at 90% speed, with a male voice for the second speaker in conversations. The download is half the size (25 MB).
- **8 new stories and 4 new conversations** for TOPIK 3–6, and reading questions in practice tests.
- **Hanja word families** on word cards (學 study: 학교, 학생, 대학교…).
- **Konglish unit:** 20 loanwords like 핸드폰, 셀카 and 화이팅.
- **Search by romanization:** "hakgyo" finds 학교.
- **Typing race:** 60 seconds, as many words as you can.
- **Smart review forecast** on the Stats page.
- **Pronunciation check** on word cards, using Windows speech recognition (needs the Korean speech pack in Windows).
- **Weekly report** notification on Sunday evening, and **What's new** after each update.
- The focus timer now rings on time even while the app sits in the tray.

## 1.4.1

- Updates now come from the main hangul-learner repository.

## 1.4.0

- First version published on GitHub, with **automatic updates**.
- Included: the Practice page (numbers, verb endings, particles, stories, role-play conversations, sound changes), the focus timer, word of the day, words from copied Korean, shadowing, dictation and word-list import.
